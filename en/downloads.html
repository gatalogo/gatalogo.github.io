Release notes

Version 1.0.0 Latest

Released on October 13, 2026

Gatálogo's first release, your personal space to record and celebrate your reading. Everything is saved on your phone: no account and no internet needed.

Library

  • Add books with cover, author, genre, pages, dates, rating, favorites, and comments.
  • Track each book as not started, in progress, finished, or abandoned.
  • Log the pages you read and see a progress bar on every book.
  • Search by title or author and filter by status, author, reading year, and period.
  • Choose a list or a grid, with 2 or 3 books per row.

Organization

  • Collections: group books any way you like.
  • Authors and genres: manage both and give each genre its own icon and color.
  • Calendar: see your reading periods month by month.
  • Timeline: see the books you started and finished, organized by month.

Statistics and achievements

  • Dashboard with an overview, reading charts, reading time, page ranking, and yearly goals.
  • Yearly recap: highlights of your year in books, ready to share.
  • Your ratings: overall average, rating distribution, and rating filters.
  • Reading streak: a calendar of consecutive reading days, with celebrations.
  • Achievements: badges you unlock as you read.

What to read next?

  • Bracket: your unstarted books compete in rounds until one wins.
  • Random: let chance pick your next read.

Sharing

  • Images ready for WhatsApp and Instagram Stories, with or without a comment.
  • Send a book file to another reader to import into their library.
  • Export a book as a Word document (.docx).

Reminders and customization

  • Streak reminder, sent only on days you have not read yet, and a weekly nudge on Sundays.
  • Dark mode, sound effects, and progress bar color (green, cover color, or app color).
  • Set the library’s starting order, view, and filter.
  • Languages: Portuguese and English.

Backup

  • Export your whole library, covers included, to a file and restore it whenever you need.
  • ⚠️ Your data stays only on this device. Back it up so you do not lose it when you change phones or uninstall the app.
