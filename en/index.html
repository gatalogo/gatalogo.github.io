📚 Features

Everything you read, in one place

From the first chapter to the last page, Gatálogo keeps your story as a reader.

Your library

Cover, author, genre, pages, dates, rating, favorites, and comments. Follow the progress of every book.

Collections and genres

Group books your way and give every genre an icon and a color.

Calendar and timeline

See when you started and finished each book, month by month.

Statistics

Charts, reading time, page ranking, yearly goals, and your yearly recap.

Reading streak

Keep your daily reading streak, with reminders that only show up when you have not read yet.

Achievements

Unlock badges as you read and celebrate every finished book.

What to read next?

Run a bracket between your unstarted books, or let chance pick.

Share

Images ready for WhatsApp and Stories, a book file for another reader, and a .docx document.

Full backup

Export your whole library, covers included, to a file and restore it whenever you need.

📱 Screens

Cozy, beautiful, and made for readers

Swipe sideways to see every screen in the app.

Your data is yours alone

Gatálogo has no server, no account, and no tracking. It does not collect or send your data: everything is saved on your device. Privacy Policy.
