1. What Gatálogo is

Gatálogo is a personal reading catalog for Android. With it you can:

  • record books with cover, author, genre, pages, dates, status, rating, favorites, and comments;
  • organize books into collections and view a reading calendar and timeline;
  • follow statistics, yearly goals, yearly recaps, a reading streak, and achievements;
  • pick your next book with a bracket or a random draw;
  • share books as an image, a file, or a Word document (.docx);
  • get reading reminders and customize how the app looks;
  • export and import backups of your library.

Gatálogo's purpose is only to catalog and organize your reading and to encourage the reading habit. The app does not let you attach, download, open, or read e-books, PDFs, ePubs, or any other file containing the content of a work. It does not distribute books and must not be used to encourage piracy or illegal reading.

The "book file" you can share contains only the record data (title, author, genre, page count, description, and cover), never the text of the work.

2. Local operation

  • data is not synced between devices. To move it to another phone, export a backup and import it on the new device.

Importing a backup replaces all current data in the app. This action cannot be undone.
